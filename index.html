How claiming works

  1. Connect the wallet that holds your Agent ID, using your own wallet app or browser wallet.
  2. Pick a drop and press Claim. Your wallet will ask you to confirm the transaction.
  3. The contract checks your Agent ID on-chain. If you hold one and have not claimed this drop yet, the badge is minted to your wallet.
  4. Nothing is charged except network gas. No payment is ever requested by this site.

Please read

  • The badge is a collectible only. No token, reward, or return of any kind is promised.
  • This site never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or pairing code. It only uses the wallet already installed in your browser, and every transaction must be confirmed by you in your own wallet.
  • One badge per wallet address per drop. The contract enforces this; there is no way around it.
  • Claiming costs network gas, which varies. Check the gas estimate in your wallet before confirming.
